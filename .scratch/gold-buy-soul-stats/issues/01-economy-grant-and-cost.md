# Economy: grant size and cost curve

## Question

What flat **Match** grant does each seat receive, and what does one **Soul** bump cost?

Decide: starting purse for both seats; cost per +1 Strength / Speed / Vitality (flat 1:1 vs escalating); whether the three stats share one price table. Keep earn/steal and non-Soul sinks out of scope.

## Answer

- **Curve:** Flat **1:1** — every +1 Strength / Speed / Vitality costs the same.
- **Price table:** Shared across all three stats (no per-stat premiums).
- **Cost:** **1** currency per +1 bump.
- **Grant:** Both seats receive **5** at Match begin (equal flat grant).
- **Implied:** Up to **5** bumps per seat this slice; soft / diminishing caps for extreme stacks stay in map fog (bumps may still exceed rolled max **10** per prior lock).
